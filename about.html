The name

Phronesis is a Greek term meaning “practical wisdom,” derived from learning and evidence of practical things.

Our name reflects our goal of providing free access to carefully selected resources that inform public understanding on topics at the intersection of technology, law, and policy.

Definition adapted from The Oxford Review.

What we do

We provide policymakers, technologists, and legal professionals with free access to primary and secondary technology policy resources, each hand-selected for relevance and quality.

Phronesis is a growing personal library, much like Westlaw and the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy were in their early days. Everything on the site has been reviewed and hand-selected by our founder, Sipho C. Langa.

Categories

Each piece belongs to one of six categories, reflecting how it was written and who it is for.

Research Papers
Academic works including working papers, preprints, and peer-reviewed publications on technology governance and digital law.
Policy Artifacts
Legislation, memos, and proposals addressed to regulators, lawmakers, and institutions.
Legal Analyses
Close readings of cases, statutes, and regulatory actions. Written for practitioners and informed general readers.
Essays
Works written to explain, argue, narrate, or describe ideas and positions on technology governance.
Articles
Timely, concise takes on current events: rulings, decisions, and policy shifts worth paying attention to right now.
Reports
Formal, organized communications that share findings from investigation, analysis, or research, tailored to a specific audience and purpose, often including recommendations or conclusions.

The founder

Phronesis was founded by Sipho C. Langa, a research engineer and legal with a background spanning economics, computer science, and public policy.

Library Acquisition Process

Every resource in the library is reviewed and hand-selected by our founder. Readers can submit topics of interest, and we will explore and curate resources on them. If you have work that fits the library, you can send it to the same address.

Send topic suggestions and submissions to submissions@phronesisresearch.org.

Independence

Phronesis is an independent, non-partisan research entity. We do not accept funding that conditions or influences our editorial positions. Our work reflects the views of its authors and no one else.

We do not compete with the institutions and publications that already exist in this space. We exist alongside them, with our own perspective and our own readers.
