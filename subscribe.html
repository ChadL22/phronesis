FAQs

How often do you send The Brief?
One email per month, typically on the first Monday. Each issue covers the prior month’s key developments in technology law and policy.
What if I miss an issue?
Every past issue is collected on this page, so you can catch up on previous months at any time.
Who produces The Brief?
The Phronesis Brief is produced in collaboration with the University of Maryland’s Tech Policy Hub, combining independent research with academic expertise.
Is my email private?
Your email address is stored securely and used only to send you The Phronesis Brief. We do not sell, trade, or share it with third parties.
How do I unsubscribe?
Enter your email address in the Subscribe box and choose “Unsubscribe.” You will be removed from the mailing list immediately.
