Frequently Asked Questions

How often do you send The Brief?
We send one email per month, typically on the first Monday of the month. Each issue covers the prior month's key developments in technology law and policy.
What are the six sections?
At Phronesis: New research and analysis. Signals: Emerging trends. Synthesis: Expert analysis. Opportunities & Deadlines: Upcoming events and key dates. Worth Reading: Curated sources. Closing Note: Reflections.
What if I miss an issue?
All past issues of The Brief are archived on our Substack page. You can browse the complete archive, search by topic, and read previous months at your convenience.
Who produces The Brief?
The Phronesis Brief is produced in collaboration with the University of Maryland's Tech Policy Hub, combining perspectives from independent research and academic expertise.
Is my email private?
Your email address is stored securely and used only for sending you The Phronesis Brief. We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties.
How do I unsubscribe?
Enter your email address above and click the Unsubscribe button. You will be removed from our mailing list immediately.
