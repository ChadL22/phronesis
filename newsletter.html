Frequently Asked Questions

How often do you send newsletters?
We send one email per week, typically on Monday mornings at 9 AM ET. Each issue includes the latest Phronesis research, analysis, and essays across all six publication formats.
What if I miss an issue?
All past newsletters are archived on our Substack page. You can browse the complete archive, search by topic, and read previous issues at your convenience.
Can I change my email address?
Yes. Simply unsubscribe using your current email address, then subscribe again with your new email.
Is my email private?
Your email address is stored securely and used only for sending you the Phronesis newsletter. We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties.
How do I contact Phronesis?
You can reach us through our About page or send an email with questions or feedback about our work.
